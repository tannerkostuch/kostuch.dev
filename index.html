02 // Selected Engineering Work

MODULES: 06 LOADED
PRJ_01 // SIM PUBLIC REPO

Alloy Discovery Simulator

A physics pipeline that hunts for new metal alloys on a desktop PC. A machine-learned interatomic potential (MACE) relaxes atomistic cells and works out stiffness and phase stability for thousands of candidates, then ranks them. Validated against 1,214 known alloys: the right crystal lattice for 99% of those with one right answer, and a 1% median density error.

  • Python
  • PyTorch
  • MACE
  • ASE
  • pymatgen
